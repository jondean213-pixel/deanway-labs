The bet

One person. One phone. A full AI lab.

DeanWay Labs is proving that frontier-grade personal AI doesn't require a data center — it requires architecture. Assistant, engineer, and governance, running locally on an ordinary phone, built in the open, owned outright by the person it serves.

We build it like it matters, because it does: every system is engineered as though it may one day become a product, documented so it can outlive us, and governed by principles that don't bend for convenience.

The Five Absolutes

These govern everything built at DeanWay Labs — including the AI running this website.

  1. Evidence comes before claims.No evidence means not verified. Never invent evidence.
  2. Built as product.Every system is engineered as though it may one day ship — repeatable, supportable, documented.
  3. No single point of failure.Nothing may depend entirely on one person being there to explain how it works.
  4. Continuity across generations.Durable knowledge lives in DeanWay-controlled systems — not in any one chat or model session.
  5. Principles before decisions.The Absolutes, architecture, and continuity records are consulted before development decisions are made.
